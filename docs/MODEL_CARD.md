# Model Card: JetPilotGuard Temporal Impairment Advisory

## Overview

A gradient-boosted classifier that estimates the probability of pilot
impairment (G-LOC risk or spatial disorientation) from a short window of
telemetry, using engineered temporal features. It powers the **ADVISORY** tier
of JetPilotGuard. It is architecturally barred from triggering an Auto-GCAS
override — only the deterministic watchdog can do that.

## Intended use

- **In scope:** early, soft warning of pilot-state degradation in the
  JetPilotGuard simulation; a demonstration of temporal modelling and
  explainability for a safety-adjacent advisory.
- **Out of scope:** any real flight operation, any real medical or
  incapacitation judgement, any certified safety function. This is a research
  and portfolio artifact trained entirely on synthetic data.

## Data

- Fully **synthetic** telemetry windows generated by
  `jetpilotguard.ml.sequence_dataset`. Each window is ~3 s (15 frames at 5 Hz).
- Four archetypes: cruise, sustained high-g, transient g-spike, disorientation.
- Labels are sampled probabilistically from a documented trajectory-based risk
  model, with the *final frame deliberately made non-discriminative* between
  the dangerous (sustained-g) and safe (transient-spike) archetypes, so that
  the signal lives in the trajectory rather than the endpoint.
- No real physiological or flight data is used. Real-world validity is unknown.

## Features

42 per-channel temporal statistics (mean, max, min, std, last, slope, delta for
each of six channels) plus four cross-channel features: cumulative G-LOC
exposure, high-g fraction, gaze-rising indicator, and perfusion-falling
magnitude. See `jetpilotguard.ml.temporal.TEMPORAL_FEATURE_NAMES`.

## Evaluation

Metrics on a held-out test split (25%), never seen in training:

| Model | ROC-AUC | Avg precision | F1 |
|-------|---------|---------------|-----|
| Per-frame baseline (final frame only) | ~0.73 | ~0.82 | ~0.81 |
| **Temporal model** | **~0.77** | **~0.87** | **~0.82** |

The temporal model wins on all five seeds tested, with a mean ROC-AUC uplift of
about **+0.037**. The comparison is fair: both models train on identical
windows and are evaluated on the identical held-out split; the baseline
receives the most recent frame (exactly what a snapshot classifier would have
at decision time).

## Explainability

Each advisory can be attributed to its top temporal drivers via SHAP
(`jetpilotguard.ml.explain.AdvisoryExplainer`), e.g. "driven by sustained high-g
exposure and falling perfusion." This is important for any advisory a human is
expected to trust.

## Limitations & ethical notes

- Synthetic data means **no claim of real-world predictive validity**.
- The model is per-window, not streaming; onset dynamics between windows are
  not modelled.
- Because it is advisory-only, a false negative does not disable the
  deterministic collision protection — a deliberate safety-architecture choice.
- Should never be represented as a medical device or certified avionics.
