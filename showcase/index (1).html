01 The core idea

Layered authority

Two failure modes threaten a fast-jet pilot: flying a good aircraft into the ground, and the pilot failing first — blacking out under G-force or losing spatial orientation. JetPilotGuard watches both, but keeps the two responses strictly separated by authority.

Layer · advises

ML impairment model

A calibrated gradient-boosted classifier estimates G-LOC and disorientation risk from pilot physiology and flight state. It raises early, soft warnings.

CAN WARN — CANNOT OVERRIDE
Layer · overrides

Deterministic watchdog

Pure kinematics compute time-to-impact from altitude, speed and flight-path angle. Every trigger condition is enumerable and unit-tested.

THE ONLY LAYER THAT SEIZES CONTROL

03 How a packet flows

Filter → advise → override → fuse

Each telemetry sample passes through the same disciplined path before a status is emitted.

01

Kalman filter

Smooths noisy altitude, g-force and perfusion signals.

02

ML advisory

Scores impairment probability from the clean signals.

03

Watchdog

Rule-based TTI on raw kinematics — no filter lag.
